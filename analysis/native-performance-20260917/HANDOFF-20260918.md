## What is settled

- **Steady two-deck playback has no stall**: 4,991 XDamage intervals, max
  20.6 ms, zero over 25 ms, zero storage reads, zero underruns.
- **The load hitch is renderer preemption.** In the first second after LOAD on
  the WAVEFORM page, with the other deck playing, the worst interval was
  53.7 / 72.0 / 51.1 ms across three baseline runs. The renderer (RR 1, the
  lowest RT priority in the process, CPU mask `3`) was runnable-but-preempted
  for ~90% of those windows by: the touchscreen I²C irq/111 thread (FIFO 50,
  ~5.8 kHz), `PageFiller0` (RR 11), `BufferingSched` (RR 44), `JUCE Timer`
  (RR 33), `Xwayland`, and our `mix-stream` (RR 8) / `aplay` (RR 10).
  Browser gaps of ~287 ms before LOAD are a list not repainting, not stalls.
- **MIT-SHM (`share_ipc`) is closed**: verified engaged, no CPU or frame-gap
  benefit under load. Keep it off.
- **irq 111 → CPU 3** removes that thread from the renderer's preemptors
  entirely and cut the worst gap to 42.4 ms in one trial (controls 51–72).
  Not applied; needs B/A/B repeats and a touch-latency check.
- **`mix-stream`/`aplay` → CPU 3**: mechanism works, **no benefit** (69.9 ms
  vs 59.5/50.0). Closed.
- Across seven load-span arms the worst gap is always at +0.53…+0.58 s after
  LOAD with `PageFiller0` (RR 11) as the top preemptor of the RR 1 renderer.
  The floor is firmware scheduling, not our layers.

- **Renderer raised to RR 12 (above `PageFiller0`)**: preemption by
  `PageFiller0` drops to zero, gap unchanged (59.1 vs 39.0/49.9). The
  renderer stops being starved and starts *waiting* (`S`/`D`). **Scheduling
  is not the root cause.** All three scheduling knobs closed.
- **What the renderer waits on (from blocked stacks + wakers, both arms):**
  `poll()` on its X socket, woken by `unix_write_space` (send buffer drained)
  and `sock_def_readable` (reply/event). It is blocked on the **X transport to
  Xwayland** during the load burst, while Xwayland (RT 1, same cores) is
  preempted by `PageFiller0`/`BufferingSched`. The ~40–70 ms gap *occurs*
  ~0.55 s after LOAD; nothing freezes for 0.55 s. Not a loader lock — that
  earlier claim was retracted once the stacks were read.
- **Quantified:** the renderer is *blocked* for 83–92% of the worst gap at
  either priority; preemption is 0–5% by time (it only dominated by count).
- **Attributed (strace + inode adjacency):** the renderer's poll loop watches
  an internal socketpair (fds 3/4) and **fd 20 = socket 115999**, one inode
  below Xwayland's accepted socket 116000 — the X connection. Direct peer
  proof still wants `ss -xe`/`lsof` (not installed). **Not recorded:** Xwayland's own
  switches — the capture filter was renderer-only and `-a` does not lift it.
- Next: prove the peer (`ss -xe`/`lsof`, or `strace -e poll,write -p MAIN`
  + `readlink /proc/MAIN/fd/N`), widen the filter to both PIDs, then A/B/A
  **renderer and Xwayland scheduled together**. Bare Xorg separate. Whether
  page-fill can defer so the waveform keeps drawing is a firmware question.

## Known limits

XDamage intervals are notification gaps, not scanout. ~1,100 intervals per
60 s carry no page sample (10 ms sampler) and are excluded. Single trials
unless stated. No listening verdict was given today. `next_comm` attribution
says who ran after the renderer was switched out, which is direct for R/R+
but silent on wakeups and D states.
